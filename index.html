Our journey

It began at a kitchen table.

What if studying felt less like a chore, and more like a daily adventure worth showing up for?

Apex started with a single printed worksheet and one restless question. A parent, an educator, and a child who found revision dull — not because the ideas were boring, but because the experience was. So we redesigned the experience.

We stripped away the noise: no endless dashboards, no pressure, no clutter. In its place we built something quieter and kinder — a programme that serves one thoughtful lesson a day, celebrates showing up, and treats a young learner's time as precious.

What grew from that table is now a full Junior GCSE companion across English, Mathematics and Science — but the heart of it hasn't changed. We still believe learning should feel like a calm, confident climb, not a scramble.

Questions

Frequently asked.

Everything you need before you begin.

Is there a free option?
Yes. The free Taster gives you today's lesson plus Unit 1 of English, Maths and Science every day — no card, no commitment. Upgrade to a paid plan whenever you're ready for the full course.
What do the plans cost, and how am I billed?
Bronze $8, Silver $12 and Gold $16 per month, in USD, billed securely through PayPal. You can cancel any time, and every plan is covered by a 14-day money-back guarantee.
What's the difference between the plans?
All paid plans unlock the full course, progressive unlocking, end-of-topic assessments and the student forum. Silver adds two exam papers marked by official examiners and a parent progress dashboard. Gold adds priority examiner feedback (within 48 hours), a monthly 1:1 tutor check-in, downloadable revision packs and priority support.
Who are the "official examiners"?
Experienced GCSE examiners who mark your submitted papers to exam-board standards and return written feedback. Silver and Gold include two marked papers; Gold receives priority turnaround.
Can parents monitor their child's progress?
Yes, on Silver and Gold. Parents get a separate, private dashboard linked to their child by a one-time code. It lives entirely in the parent's own login — the child's study screen shows nothing about monitoring, so it never disrupts learning.
Is my child's data safe?
Always. We show no ads and never sell data. Payments are handled by PayPal, so we never see card details. Learners under 16 should be set up by a parent or guardian, who provides consent. See our Privacy Policy.
How does sign-up work?
Create an account, verify it with a one-time code sent to your email, then choose a plan and pay securely with PayPal. Your access unlocks the moment payment is confirmed.
Which exam boards do you cover?
Content carries indicative spec references for AQA, Edexcel and OCR. Always check your board's current official specification when planning revision.
