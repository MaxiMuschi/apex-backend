How It Works

  1. One subject per day

    The journal rotates through the subjects across the week, with a review day. You don’t choose — the schedule does. Mondays & Thursdays are English, Tuesdays & Fridays Maths, Wednesdays & Saturdays Science, and Sunday is review.

  2. Open the app, do today’s lesson

    Each lesson takes about 30 minutes: a short concept reading, two worked examples, and five practice questions you check yourself.

  3. Print if you prefer paper

    Every lesson has a “Print worksheet” button that strips the navigation and produces a clean, A4-ready page.

  4. Progress builds itself

    Completion is saved automatically. A streak counter rewards showing up daily. Finish the schedule and the full foundation is covered.

  5. For parents & teachers

    No account, no login, nothing to manage. The journal lives in the browser tab.
