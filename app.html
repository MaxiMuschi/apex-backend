How It Works

  1. One subject per day

    The journal rotates through the subjects across the week, with a review day. You don’t choose — the schedule does. Mondays & Thursdays are English, Tuesdays & Fridays Maths, Wednesdays & Saturdays Science, and Sunday is review.

  2. Open the app, do today’s lesson

    Each lesson takes about 30 minutes: a short concept reading, two worked examples, and exam-style practice questions you check yourself — with mark schemes.

  3. Set your board and tier

    Choose your exam board (AQA, Edexcel, OCR) and tier (Foundation or Higher). Maths and Combined Science are tiered; English Language is untiered, so the tier setting doesn’t change it. Spec references update to match your board.

  4. Print if you prefer paper

    Every lesson has a “Print worksheet” button that strips the navigation, mark schemes and model answers to produce a clean, A4-ready worksheet.

  5. Progress builds itself

    Completion is saved automatically. A streak counter rewards showing up daily. No account, no login, nothing to manage — the journal lives in the browser tab.

The Study Journal · runs entirely in your browser · nothing is sent to a server.

Spec references are indicative and should be checked against your board’s current official specification.
